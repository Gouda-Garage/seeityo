Privacy

Short, because there's nothing to hide.

We collect nothing.

See It Yo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers receiving anything from your phone. We don't know who you are or what you looked at. The app doesn't even ask Android for internet permission.

The camera.

The app uses your camera live, to show you a bigger picture of what you point it at. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Frames exist while you're looking and are gone when you close the app. When you freeze the image, that frozen frame lives in memory only and disappears when you resume or close.

This website.

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, and fonts are served from this site. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under its own privacy policy. The browser version uses your camera the same way: live, in memory, never uploaded.

Feedback.

The feedback form doesn't send anything from the website. It opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing goes out unless you press send there.

Downloads.

The Android test build is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub handles that download under its own privacy policy.

Questions.

Ask the person who sent you the link, or email werd@mopedmotel.com. This is a one-person project.
