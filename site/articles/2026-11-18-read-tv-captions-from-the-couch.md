<div class="answer">Two fixes. First, make the text bigger at the source in your TV or streaming app's caption settings. Second, when you can't, zoom in on the screen with your phone and freeze the frame.</div>

You shouldn't have to walk up to the TV to read the score.

## Fix 1: make the TV do the work

Most TVs and streaming apps let you change caption size, color and background. Look for **Accessibility**, **Captions** or **Subtitles** in settings. Bigger text with a solid background is easier to read than the default.

## Fix 2: zoom with your phone

1. Sit where you normally sit.
2. Open your camera and zoom until the text is readable.
3. Rest your elbows on your knees to keep steady.
4. Freeze the frame if your app allows it, so you can read it without holding the pose.

## What makes it harder

- **Distance.** The farther you are, the more you zoom, and the softer the picture gets.
- **Dark room.** The TV is the only light, so the camera works harder. Rest the phone on something solid.
- **Screen flicker.** Cameras can catch the screen refreshing. Tilt the phone slightly or change the zoom a notch.
