<div class="answer">Reading glasses win for long reading. The camera app is fine and free. A one-tap magnifier app wins when you want it fast, in the dark, or across a room.</div>

Three honest options, and when each makes sense.

| | Reading glasses | Camera app | Magnifier app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | $ to $$ | Free, already there | Free to a few dollars |
| Speed | Instant, if you have them | Several taps | Often one tap |
| Hands-free | Yes | No | No |
| Dark rooms | Need light | Flashlight by hand | Some turn the light on for you |
| Far away (TV, sign) | No | Yes, with zoom | Yes, with zoom |
| Always with you | Only if you carry them | Yes | Yes |

## Reading glasses

Best for menus, books and anything you read for more than a minute. The weakness is that they're in the other jacket.

## Camera app

Free and already on your phone. The cost is fiddling: open, zoom, tap to focus, find the flashlight.

## A magnifier app

Skips the fiddling. How well it works depends on the app and your phone. Some can freeze the frame, some can light the page for you, and some do extra things like reading text aloud.

## Where See It Yo fits

[See It Yo](../../) is one option in the third column, and it's still a beta. It opens already zoomed, turns the light on by itself when it's dark and freezes the picture on a tap.
