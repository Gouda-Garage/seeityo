<div class="answer">Most phone zoom is digital zoom, which crops the picture. Fewer real pixels means softer text. Move closer first, zoom second, add light, and hold still.</div>

If you've zoomed in on a menu and watched the words turn to mush, here's what is going on.

## Digital vs. optical zoom

- **Digital zoom** crops the middle of the picture and enlarges it. The more you zoom, the fewer real pixels you have, so the image gets soft.
- **Optical zoom** uses a separate telephoto lens to get a closer view without cropping. Some phones have one, many don't, and it still has limits.

## Five things that help

1. **Move closer, then zoom less.** Distance is free sharpness.
2. **Add light.** Bright light lets the camera use a faster shutter and less noise cleanup.
3. **Hold still.** Rest your elbows on a table or tuck them into your body.
4. **Tap to focus on the print.** Don't let it focus on the table behind it.
5. **Freeze the frame if you can.** Then your shaky hands don't matter.
