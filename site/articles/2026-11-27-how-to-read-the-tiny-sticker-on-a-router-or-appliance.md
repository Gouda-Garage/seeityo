The one number you need is always printed on the smallest sticker, in the worst spot: behind the TV, under the router, or on the back of a dishwasher.

## What you may find there

Stickers often show a model number, a serial number and sometimes the default Wi-Fi name and password. Layouts differ, so check what yours says. This is the information customer support usually asks for.

## Read it without moving things

You often don't need to pull the device out. Hold your phone a hand's width away, zoom in on the sticker and turn on the light. Tilt the phone so the light doesn't bounce off the glossy label.

## Freeze, then copy it down

Tricky spots are hard to hold still in. Freeze the picture, then write the numbers on paper or type them into a note. Read each character twice, since a zero and the letter O can look alike at small sizes.

## Be careful with heavy or hot gear

If you do need to move something, follow its manual, let hot devices cool, and ask a person for help with heavy appliances. Don't climb or stretch to read a label.

## Quick checklist

- Zoom from a distance
- Light the gap, tilt to avoid glare
- Freeze the picture
- Double-check letters that look like numbers
