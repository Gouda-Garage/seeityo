<div class="answer">Read your ticket in the lobby first and remember your row and seat. In the dark, aim a low phone light at the seat number from the side and zoom in so you don't have to lean in.</div>

You walk in, the trailers have started, and the row letters are the size of a postage stamp. Here is how to find your seat without bothering the whole room.

## Do the work in the lobby

The easiest fix is before you go in. Zoom in on the ticket, find your row and seat, and say them out loud once. Then you only need to match two things in the dark.

## Use the aisle lights

Most theaters have small lights along the steps and row ends. Let those do the work. Walk down until you see your row letter, then look along the row ends for the number.

## If you need your phone

Keep the light low and aim it down at the number, from the side, not toward the screen. Zoom in so the print fills the picture, then glance and turn it off. A quick look is polite. A long one is not.

## Freeze, don't wave

If you need to read your ticket again, freeze the picture so you can read it with the phone pointed at the floor. [See It Yo](../../) turns on the light only when it's dark and freezes the frame on one tap, so you can read once and put the phone away.

For other dim places, see [how to read a menu in a dark restaurant](../read-a-menu-in-a-dark-restaurant/).

## Quick checklist

- Read the ticket in the lobby
- Use the aisle lights
- Phone light low, aimed down, brief
- Phone away before the movie starts
